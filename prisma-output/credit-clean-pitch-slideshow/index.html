Credit Clean: self-service debt
resolution inside the tools merchants already use

What it does
  • + Shows merchants their exact debt, broken down by contract
  • + Presents renegotiation options from the existing credit engine
  • + Pairs debt data with LCM business health context
  • + Available 24/7 -- no call center, no appointments
What it does not do
  • - Does not approve new loans
  • - Does not modify credit scores
  • - Does not change merchant visibility to consumers
  • - Does not build a new credit engine

The 60-second merchant journey

1
Trigger
Non-blocking notification appears in Gestor de Pedidos. Does not interrupt order flow.
2
View
Debt summary + LCM business health snapshot. "Your Acai de 500ml is trending up 31%."
3
Options
Renegotiation plans from the existing credit engine. Each shows monthly amount and total cost.
4
Commit
Merchant selects a plan and confirms. Status updates immediately.
5
Track
Repayment progress shown alongside business metrics. Each on-time payment is acknowledged.

Three things changed that make this timely

1
LCM merchant profiles went live (Jan 2026)
AI-generated business profiles for every merchant -- commercial health, conversion funnels, trending items. We can now contextualize debt within business trajectory.
2
Credit portfolio data infrastructure is mature
movilepay_credit_curated pipeline tracks contracts, overdue balances, renegotiations, installments. The data exists; the merchant-facing experience does not.
3
Churn from unresolved debt is now measurable
merchants_history tracks status transitions including CHURN. The cost of inaction shows up in two ledgers: lost debt recovery and lost future GMV.
